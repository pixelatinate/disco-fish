# Description
Your scene must have no less than 3 objects:
X Goldfish Model
X Egg Model
X Bubbles Model
Your scene must have no less than 3 effects:
X Texture Reflection on the Bubbles
X Rainbow Egg Effect
X Cube Map on the Environment
X Normal Bump Mapping on the Fish Model
Your renderer must use no less than 3 different fragment shaders:
X Rainbow Glass Shading on the Egg
X "Gold" (Copper!) on the Goldfish
X "Water" Shading on the Bubbles
X Both Phong and Gourad shading on the skybox
